Status: DRAFT. Nothing has been created in Apollo or sent. Fill the [BRACKETS] with real details before use. Do not claim results or clients you cannot back up.

## Rules for this sequence
- Send from a warmed mailbox on a secondary domain, not weareninetytwo.com. Keep volume at 30 to 50 a day per mailbox at first.
- Include a plain-text unsubscribe line and your physical business address in every email (CAN-SPAM).
- Only contact business addresses. Remove anyone who replies "no" or "unsubscribe" the same day.
- Keep each email under 90 words, plain text, no images, no attachments, one link at most.
- Personalise the first line with one true, specific observation. Do not fake it.
- Stop the sequence when someone replies.

## Subject line tests
Test two subject lines per segment: one with the company name, one with a plain question ("who handles your vehicle graphics?"). Keep the winner after about 100 sends.

## Reply handling
- Positive: reply within 2 hours with two time slots and a link to a booking page.
- "Not now": ask when to check back, and set a task.
- "Wrong person": ask who the right contact is.
- "Stop" or unsubscribe: remove immediately and suppress the address.

## Metrics to track (weekly)
Emails sent, bounce rate (keep under 3%), reply rate (target 3 to 5%), positive replies, calls booked, proposals, wins. If bounces exceed 3%, pause and clean the list.
